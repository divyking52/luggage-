24/7 Human Dispatch

Have a bespoke itinerary?

Multi-hotel group transfers, oversized ski/golf gear, or private aviation tarmac handoffs—our specialists arrange it directly.

01How does luggage delivery work?Delivery
Reserve your pickup and drop-off points online in under 60 seconds. A uniformed ROVÉ courier meets you at your airport arrivals hall, hotel bell desk, or residence, verifies your booking ID, photographs bag condition, locks each zipper with a serialized NFC tamper-evident seal, and delivers directly to your destination on schedule.
02Where can I store my luggage?Vaults
ROVÉ operates dedicated climate-controlled vaults and vetted 5-star hotel concierge storage lounges minutes from major airports, high-speed rail terminals, and cultural districts across London, Paris, Dubai, New York, Singapore, and Tokyo.
03Is my luggage insured?Security
Every sealed bag handled by ROVÉ is protected by our comprehensive chain-of-custody protocol—including multi-angle intake photography, serialized NFC tamper seals, dedicated enclosed courier vehicles, and per-bag protection coverage outlined transparently before checkout.
04Can I change my booking?Flexibility
Yes. You can modify pickup times, update destination addresses, add additional bags, or cancel free of charge up to 2 hours prior to your scheduled collection window directly from your confirmation pass.
05How do I track my luggage?Tracking
As soon as your bags are sealed, you receive a unique ROVÉ Tracking ID with live GPS corridor updates and five verified custody checkpoints—from collection to signed hotel bell-desk or terminal handoff.
06What happens if my luggage is delayed?Delays
Inbound flight delays are tracked automatically via your flight number at no extra fee. For outbound airport deliveries, we build a minimum 90-minute safety buffer before your check-in window and dispatch priority backup couriers on congested corridors.
07Are there luggage size restrictions?Sizing
Standard Comfort and Essential bookings welcome carry-ons, large check-in spinners (up to 32 kg / 70 lbs), backpacks, and garment bags. Oversized trunks, golf clubs, skis, surfboards, and strollers are supported under our Premium tier or Oversized Add-On.
08Can I book same-day delivery?Express
Absolutely. Same-day collections can be booked with as little as 90 minutes' notice across central city zones and airport terminals, with non-stop Express courier options available when every minute matters.

Delivery FAQs

Do I need to be at the hotel when bags arrive?
No—if your hotel has a 24-hour reception or bell desk, we hand off your sealed luggage against your reservation name and send you photographic confirmation immediately.
How early before my flight should bags arrive at the airport?
Select a terminal handoff time 2 to 3 hours prior to departure. Your courier meets you right outside your airline's check-in zone so you can check bags in seconds.
Can you transport golf clubs, skis, or trunks?
Yes—simply choose our Premium tier or add an Oversized Gear tag during booking so we dispatch a high-capacity van.

  • Hourly & daily options with automatic best-rate cap

Can I convert stored bags into an airport delivery later?
Yes! Tap “Dispatch to Airport” on your digital vault pass at least 3 hours before your flight and a ROVÉ courier will bring your stored bags straight to departures.

Stage 01 • Booking Process

60-Second Flight-Synced Reservation

Enter your pickup terminal, hotel name, or city vault alongside your flight code. Our dispatch engine automatically adjusts courier arrival if your aircraft lands early or hits headwinds.

Photographic Check & NFC Seal

Your uniformed ROVÉ concierge verifies your digital pass, captures high-resolution exterior condition photos, and threads a tamper-evident serialized NFC seal through every zipper pull.

Live Corridor Telemetry

Stage 04 • Delivery Process

Signed Bell-Desk or Terminal Handoff

At hotels, we log a signed digital receipt with the head concierge so your bags await in your room. At airports, we meet you directly outside departures check-in.
